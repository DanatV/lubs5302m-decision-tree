Choosing a University for Master's Study

Build a decision tree representing how you would choose between universities for Master's study. The five factors below are provided, but you determine how important they are, where they appear in your tree, and the criteria used to make each decision.

Decision-tree terminology

Decision node: a factor used to make a decision.

Branch: the condition or rule determining which route through the tree is followed.

Leaf: the outcome reached at the end of a route through the tree.

About this exercise

This exercise demonstrates that a decision tree is not simply a visual representation of factors that matter. It requires the decision-maker to establish a hierarchy of decisions, formulate rules that determine different routes through the tree, and define the outcomes that follow from those rules. Different people can construct very different trees for the same decision.

Your decision tree is stored only in this browser. No information is uploaded or submitted.
